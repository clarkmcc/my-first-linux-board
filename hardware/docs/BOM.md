# Bill of materials

This is the final fitted hardware BOM. The three U1 schematic units are one physical F1C200S package. H1-H4 are fabricated mounting holes rather than purchased electronic parts.

The nominal regulated targets are 1.131707 V core, 2.556098 V DDR, 3.404878 V I/O, and 2.8 V analog. Preserve the stated precision and temperature coefficient for the regulator feedback and supervisor resistors.
